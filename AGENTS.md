# energy-aware-llvm

A dual-objective, energy-aware C/C++ compiler framework combining single-pass front-end lowering to reduce compilation energy (E_compile) with ML- and search-guided LLVM pass selection to minimize runtime energy and the Energy-Delay Product (EDP).   

## Layout

- `benchmarks/`
- `docs/`
- `energy/`
- `frontend/`
- `ml_models/`
- `reports/`
- `tests/`
- `web-app/`
